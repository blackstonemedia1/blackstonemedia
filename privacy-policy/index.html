Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Aug 15, 2026

Black Stone Media (“Black Stone Media,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit blackstonemedia.io, contact us, or use our services.

By using our website or engaging our services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address and phone number
  • Company name and business information
  • Website URL
  • Information submitted through contact or inquiry forms
  • Project requirements and communications
  • Content, files, credentials, or other information provided for project delivery
  • Billing and payment information

We may also automatically collect certain technical information, including your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, referring website, and general website activity.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and service requests
  • Prepare proposals and quotes
  • Deliver and manage our services
  • Communicate with clients about projects and services
  • Process payments and maintain business records
  • Improve our website, services, and user experience
  • Analyze website traffic and performance
  • Measure marketing and advertising performance
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, or unauthorized activity
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies, analytics tools, pixels, and similar technologies to understand website usage, improve functionality, measure marketing performance, and support advertising activities.

These technologies may include services such as Google Analytics, Google Ads, and Meta advertising tools.

You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain website functionality.

4. Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers or financial institutions.

Black Stone Media does not store complete credit card or banking credentials on our website servers. Payment information is handled by the applicable payment provider according to its own privacy and security practices.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share information with trusted service providers when reasonably necessary to operate our website, deliver services, process payments, provide analytics, or support our business operations.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or when necessary to protect our rights, property, users, or business.

6. Data Security

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal and project information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and satisfy applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

When information is no longer reasonably required, we may securely delete or anonymize it.

8. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, platforms, or services.

Black Stone Media does not control and is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to information we maintain about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain information
  • Request restriction of certain processing activities
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

To submit a privacy-related request, contact us at inquiry@blackstonemedia.io.

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are intended for individuals 18 years of age or older.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business, services, technology, or applicable laws.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact:

Black Stone Media
Email: inquiry@blackstonemedia.io
Website: blackstonemedia.io

Refund Policy

Last Updated: Aug 15, 2026

At Black Stone Media, we provide customized digital marketing, SEO, web design and development, advertising, and lead generation services. This Refund Policy outlines the terms applicable to payments made for our services.

1. Refund Eligibility

To provide clients with an opportunity to review their purchase, refund requests may be submitted within 3 calendar days of the original payment.

After this 3-day period, the applicable service or product is considered non-refundable.

Where work has already commenced during this period, the refund may be subject to the work or resources already provided.

2. Ongoing Services

This includes:

  • SEO & Local SEO
  • LinkedIn Lead Generation
  • Social Media Marketing
  • Digital Marketing
  • Google & Meta Advertising
  • Ongoing marketing services

For recurring services, the 3-day refund period applies to the initial payment or applicable new service purchase. Future billing cycles may be cancelled according to the terms of the applicable service agreement.

Once the applicable refund period has passed, fees for the active billing period are non-refundable.

3. Website & Development Projects

This includes:

  • Website Design
  • Website Development
  • Website Redesign
  • E-commerce Development
  • Related development services

Any refund request must be submitted within 3 calendar days of payment.

Once the 3-day period has passed, payments associated with the project are non-refundable, including deposits, milestone payments, and other project fees.

4. Advertising Spend

Advertising budgets allocated to third-party platforms such as Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, or other advertising platforms are separate from Black Stone Media’s service fees.

Advertising spend is subject to the policies of the respective advertising platform and is not refundable by Black Stone Media once allocated or spent.

5. Third-Party Costs

Costs incurred on behalf of a client through third-party services, including domains, hosting, software, subscriptions, plugins, advertising platforms, or other external services, are generally non-refundable and subject to the respective provider’s terms.

6. Client Responsibilities

Project timelines may depend on the client providing required information, content, access, approvals, and feedback in a timely manner.

Delays caused by missing information, delayed approvals, or client-requested changes do not automatically qualify for a refund.

7. How to Request a Refund

Refund requests must be submitted within the applicable 3-day refund period.

Please contact: inquiry@blackstonemedia.io

Include:

  • Full name
  • Company name
  • Invoice or order number
  • Service purchased
  • Payment date
  • Reason for the refund request

Black Stone Media will review eligible requests and generally respond within 2–3 business days.

8. Service Agreements

Where a separate proposal, statement of work, or service agreement has been signed, its specific payment and cancellation terms will apply.

In the event of a conflict between this policy and a signed agreement, the signed agreement will control to the extent permitted by applicable law.

9. Policy Updates

Black Stone Media may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date.

10. Contact Us

For questions regarding this Refund Policy:

Black Stone Media
Email: inquiry@blackstonemedia.io
Website: blackstonemedia.io
